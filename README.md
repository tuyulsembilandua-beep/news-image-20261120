One image summing up the world's news of Friday 20 November 2026: a newspaper front page with four stories — the first-ever Trump–Xi–Putin trilateral summit in Shenzhen, the ICJ ruling that Myanmar committed genocide against the Rohingya, Cyclone Akvan bearing down on India's Odisha coast with 300,000 evacuated, and Voyager 1 reaching one light-day from Earth — plus a ticker with six more stories of the day (Trump delaying the Iran decision to 2027, Europe open to Ukraine talks, Russian strikes damaging EU embassies in Kyiv, GTA 6 outselling The Odyssey, Hayabusa2's landing called off, Kushner on Article 5 guarantees).

The image is built at build time (generated panel artwork composed with PIL into `entry.png` by `compose.py`); `main.py` just copies its bytes to `/output/entry.png`, so the program is deterministic and needs no network.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
```
